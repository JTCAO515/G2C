Two agents, one trip that just works.

CJ Agent

Answers questions. Plans your days. Helps you act.

  • Answers

    How to pay, catch a train or book a ticket — practical answers with sources you can check.

  • Plans

    Build a daily plan around your interests, budget and pace. Keep confirmed arrangements safe as the trip changes.

  • Acts

    Help with bookings and follow-up. Anything that spends money or changes your itinerary waits for your OK.

JT Agent

A person steps in when the trip needs more than an answer.

  1. Flags a changed flight, a missing hotel booking or an unresolved question.

  2. Creates a private case with only the information needed to resolve it.

  3. A teammate checks the details and contacts the hotel, venue or other provider.

  4. The outcome and next step come back to your chat.

From the Great Wall to the Bund, it knows the way.

Your details stay yours.

Names, passport numbers, phone numbers, emails and exact locations are swapped for placeholders on our side before any model reads them.

  • Teammates see masked cases under a random case ID, never your account.
  • Revealing a field is limited to what the task needs, time-boxed and logged.
  • Your memory is yours: see it, correct it, delete it whenever you like.
