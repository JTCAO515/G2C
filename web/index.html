Two agents, one trip that just works.

CJ Agent

Talks with you. Thinks ahead. Takes action.

  • Answers

    Payments, transit, tickets, food — clear answers in plain English, with sources you can check.

  • Plans

    Day-by-day itineraries shaped by what it remembers about you, and kept safe once you confirm them.

  • Acts

    Turns the plan into done — and anything that spends money or changes your trip waits for your OK.

JT Agent

Watches your back, and brings in a real person when AI isn't enough.

  1. Spots what needs attention — a changed flight, a hotel that can't find you, a question going in circles.

  2. Opens a private case with only the details that step needs.

  3. A teammate verifies, calls, confirms — no queue, no “please hold”.

  4. You simply get the result, back in your chat.

From the Great Wall to the Bund, it knows the way.

Your details stay yours.

Names, passport numbers, phone numbers, emails and exact locations are swapped for placeholders on our side before any model reads them.

  • Teammates see masked cases under a random case ID, never your account.
  • Revealing a field is limited to what the task needs, time-boxed and logged.
  • Your memory is yours: see it, correct it, delete it whenever you like.
