# Lio 陌生人测试 / First-time Lio tasks

## 中文

1. 问 Lio 明天北京的天气，找到预报来源。
2. 查从故宫走到景山公园大概要多久，找到路线来源。
3. 让 Lio 为两个人规划从明天开始的北京三天游，第一天 9:00–10:00 的酒店集合已经定好。找到保存好的三天行程。
4. 让 Lio 把第二天下午换成颐和园，保留其他安排。确认酒店集合时间照旧，再撤销这次修改。
5. 让 Lio 记住你喜欢慢节奏旅行。开一段新对话，看看它是否记得。删除这条偏好，再开一段新对话检查。

## English

1. Ask Lio about tomorrow’s weather in Beijing and find the forecast source.
2. Find out how long it takes to walk from the Palace Museum to Jingshan Park and find the route source.
3. Ask Lio to plan three days in Beijing for two people starting tomorrow. The hotel meetup from 9 to 10 a.m. on the first day is fixed. Find the saved three-day itinerary.
4. Ask Lio to change the second afternoon to the Summer Palace and keep the other plans. Check that the hotel meetup stayed the same, then undo the change.
5. Have Lio remember that you prefer a slow pace when you travel. See if it remembers in a new chat. Delete that preference and check again in another new chat.
